New in 4.0: your team in one app

YOUR TEAM AND YOUR ACCOUNT
• Create an account to see your team's team match right away and join with one tap.
• A notification when a match of your team starts and when it is decided.
• Matches someone else records for you arrive in your app by themselves. You choose whether they count.
• Add a photo to your account; your teammates see it with you.

LEAGUE
• Season per player in My team: the SBN standings and what the app knows from the team matches.
• New badges: Team player in bronze, silver and gold, and Match winner.

ALSO
• A calmer home screen, with match requests when something is waiting for you.
• Ask a question about the app with the speech bubble; the answer comes from the manual.
