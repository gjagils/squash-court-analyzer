User guide

For every part of the app we explain what you can do with it and how it works: refereeing, coaching and sharing. The guide describes version 3.0. Choose your device: the iPhone and Android apps have the same screens, only the backup works differently.

iPhone

Refereeing and coaching, Analysis and Coach card, League with a live team page, players and badges, and a weekly backup in iCloud.

Android

Refereeing and coaching, Analysis and Coach card, League with a live team page, players and badges, and backups to a folder you choose.
