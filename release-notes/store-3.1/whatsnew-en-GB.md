MY TEAM
• Find your club and tap your team: the standings, your next match and your opponent's standing on the home screen, without copying a link.
• In a team match, pick the opposing players with one tap.

COACHING
• Simple coaching: just tap who won the point and where on the court; the analysis shows where points are won and lost.
• Practice match: kept, but without badges and outside your player profile.
• Shorter match setup; the last three points sit as a list under the score.

NEW LOOK
• A calmer home screen and a tidied colour palette: sea green for good news and everything live, red only for errors, gold only for badges.
• In the Analysis you see at a glance what went well and what cost points.

ALSO
