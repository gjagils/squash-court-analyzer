Squash Analyzer helps you keep track of squash matches as a coach or referee. Record the score, spot patterns and share the result. Free and without ads. An account is optional, but makes things much easier with your team.

YOUR TEAM AND YOUR ACCOUNT (NEW IN 4.0)
• When someone on your team starts a team match, it shows in your app right away and you join with one tap.
• You get a notification when a match of your team starts and when it is decided.
• When a teammate, coach or referee records your match, it arrives in your app by itself. You choose whether it counts.
• As a bonus those matches count for your badges, also on a new phone, and your photo shows with you in your teammates' app.
• Create an account with your email address or with Apple, ages 16 and up. You can always delete it in the app.

REFEREE
• Keep the score, lets and strokes, with Undo and a rally-by-rally score line.
• The server and service box are tracked for you; set the preferred box per player.
• Official timers for the warm-up, the minute to the start and the 2 minutes between games; your phone vibrates at the side change, at 15 seconds and at Time.
• Join a match later or complete a result that was not tracked yet.
• Share the score live with a link, and follow your own match on your lock screen.

COACH
• Record winners, forced errors, unforced errors, strokes and service points.
• Or coach the simple way: just tap who won the point and where on the court. You can switch between points.
• Too fast? Tap who scored and then Didn't see it: the score carries on.
• Choose a court with 6 or 9 zones and the shots that fit the chosen row: Drop, Boast, Kill, Drive, Cross or Lob. Mark a volley with the Volley switch.
• After each game, see the Analysis per player, zone and shot, with tactical advice and the last points in a row.
• Show your player the Coach card between games: three coaching points and what changed since the last game.

LEAGUE
• Keep track of a whole team match, from E1 to E4, with the winner and bonus points under the Dutch squash federation (SBN) rules.
• Link a match you scored as coach or referee, or enter the game scores.
• Share the team match live: teammates add their own match and supporters follow the whole evening.
• When you take part in a team match, it appears on your home screen with the score and E1 to E4.

MY TEAM
• Find your club and tap your team: the standings, your next match and your opponent's standing on the home screen.
• The match list shows which match is live; one tap turns a coming match into a team match.
• Your teammates are ready in Players, and you pick the opposing players with one tap.

LIVE SHARING AND LOCK SCREEN
• Let others follow a match live through a browser link, with player photos if you like.
• Follow a match or team match on your lock screen, even with the app closed, with a notification for every game and the result.
• Live data is stored in the EU only and deleted 2 hours after the last score update.

PLAYERS AND BADGES
• Save players with a photo and coaching notes and pick them for a new match.
• View your opponent's profile, see the head-to-head record in Choose player and open the ranking with a SquashLevels link.
• Earn badges with saved players: 39 badges, eighteen of them in bronze, silver and gold, with the new Team player and Match winner for the league. See when they were earned and share a player card.

SHARE AND KEEP
• Share a Scorecard, Report or Image.
• Save matches, resume them and look back at finished matches.
• Make a backup to iCloud Drive by hand or automatically every week. Backups can be exchanged with the Android version.
• Choose a light or dark appearance, or let your phone decide.
• A question about the app? Tap the speech bubble on the home screen; the answer comes from the manual.

The app follows your phone's language: English, or Dutch when your phone is set to Dutch.
