Delete account

A SquashAnalyzer player account (from version 4.0) is never required. Don't want one any more? This is how you delete it, with everything in it.

phone_iphoneIn the app

Open Settings → My account and tap Delete account. If you signed in with Apple, the app asks you to sign in with Apple once more, so we can revoke the access at Apple.

mailWithout the app

No longer have the app? Send an email from your account's email address to info@squashanalyzer.com with the subject Delete account. If you signed in with Apple using a hidden address, mention your player name. We delete the account within a week and email you when it is done.

deleteWhat is deleted

  • Your account and your Firebase login (email address, password or Sign in with Apple)
  • Your player name, your SBN player page and team, your birth year and your consent
  • All matches and badges in your account, and the requests in your inbox
  • The live team matches you showed to your team through your account

This happens right away. The database backup may still hold it for at most 30 days; after that it is gone everywhere. What is on your own phone, such as your players and matches, stays there until you delete it yourself. More in the privacy policy.
