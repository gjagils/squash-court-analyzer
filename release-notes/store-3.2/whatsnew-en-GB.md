New in 3.2

LEAGUE
• When you take part in a team match, it appears on your home screen with the score and E1 to E4. One tap and you referee or coach your match.
• My team shows which match is live; a tap opens the team match or creates one from a coming match.
• The opposing players also arrive on a phone without its own My team.

REFEREE
• Official timers: warm-up, 1 minute to the start and 2 minutes between games. Your phone vibrates at the side change, at 15 seconds and at Time.

COACHING
• Did it go too fast? Tap who scored and then Didn't see it: the score carries on.
• You can now also choose Detailed or Simple between points.

KNOW YOUR OPPONENT
• View your opponent's profile and see the head-to-head record in Choose player. With a SquashLevels link you can also open the ranking.

ALSO
• A better display on some Android phones, and adding your team is easier.
