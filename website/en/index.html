Two modes, one app

For every role on the squash court

SquashAnalyzer has a separate mode for referees who want to score quickly and by the rules, and for coaches who want to record every rally.

sports

Referee Mode

Fast, professional scoring with the official serving rules. Best of 5, with let calls, strokes and a score line that shows every rally.

  • Service box switches automatically after every rally won
  • Lock Left/Right per player (e.g. left-handed)
  • Let call & stroke recording
  • Live sharing with a link, including player photos
  • Your own match on your lock screen too
  • Rally-by-rally score line between the players
  • Match and game timer, undo last point
  • Share the result via WhatsApp: Scorecard, Report or Image
assignment

Coach Mode

Record every rally in detail: tap the score of whoever wins the point, choose the point type, the zone on the court and the shot. The app keeps track of who serves and from which box. After the game you get the Analysis and the Coach card to show your player what matters.

  • Winner, forced error, unforced error, stroke or service point
  • For an unforced error, also the kind of error: down, out, serve or floor
  • START GAME at the first serve
  • Court in 6 zones, or 9 if you prefer
  • Per zone only the shots that fit, including Kill
  • "Volley" switch for shots on the volley
  • Analysis per game: heatmap, shots, point breakdown, serve and return, and tactical advice
  • Coach card between games: three coaching points and what changed
  • A note per game, typed or dictated
  • AI coaching advice with your own OpenAI key
  • Let call, rally timer and undo
  • Share the score as a scorecard, report or image
  • Live sharing: share a link and your team follows the score in the browser or on the lock screen
  • Save, resume and review matches, including the Coach card

Privacy

Your players and matches are stored on your phone. Online features use the internet: AI Coach receives statistics without names, My team fetches SBN data, and you can import teams or share data. Live data is deleted from the server 2 hours after the last score update.

Badges

Players you pick with "Choose player" earn badges automatically during matches: in a single game, in a single match and across all their matches. There are 37, from 5 points in a row to a two-minute rally; seventeen badges have a bronze, silver and gold tier. Share the player card with a link.
